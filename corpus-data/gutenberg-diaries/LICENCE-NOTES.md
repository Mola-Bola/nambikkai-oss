## What is here (plain text + PG metadata per book)
1. `pepys-diary-complete-1660-1669/` — Diary of Samuel Pepys, Complete (Wheatley ed.), PG #4200,

   17th-c. per-person timeline; pairs with Pepys for cross-person same-era patterns.
